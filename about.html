To me, architecture begins with a condition that refuses to be filed away — a material used outside its intended role, a position that won’t resolve into either side. I set out to embrace the what-ifs beyond the hidden boundaries. I find myself noticing the unseen.

Architecture here is gradually understood through experience, where meaning is not "given" but emerges through navigation and unconscious association.

Space here sustains ambiguity, allowing perception and movement to remain in constant flux. There is a deep fascination with the production of psychological resonance and how spatial sequences can reveal sensations that exist before they are even named. Ultimately, the intent is to create spaces that do not merely deliver a message, but rather provide a framework where inner states can be spatially encountered and where the boundary between the physical and the experiential is intentionally blurred.
