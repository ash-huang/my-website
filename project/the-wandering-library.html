The library reclines quietly on the ground, embracing the sea view and creating seamless connectivity between the seaside and the library interior with gently sloping pathways. A simple yet elegant façade design enables a smooth spatial flow between indoor and outdoor environments. The experience of wandering begins outdoors and continues naturally inside, where expansive vertical spaces are interlaced with intersecting ramps. These ramps form a singular, continuous itinerary that guides visitors from the ground floor to the top floor, fostering an intuitive and dynamic circulation pattern. Along the ramps, glass-enclosed exhibition rooms and discussion spaces are suspended, blending circulation with exhibition, fostering a multifaceted environment for activities, exploration, and exchange.
