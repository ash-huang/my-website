"Perceptual Paradox" is a holographic spatial experiment that merges non-Euclidean geometry with computational shader optics. Utilizing an impossible 3D Penrose Triangle intersected by an encircling ring and an absurdist pineapple tank, the project leverages custom GLSL shaders—combining Rembrandt directional lighting with Gooch warm/cool chromatic shading—to manipulate stereoscopic perception. Rendered for the Looking Glass multiview display via a 7×7 quilt matrix (49 discrete views), the work challenges conventional depth cues and prompts viewers to reconsider the boundaries between visual perception and physical reality.
