This project is my first studio exploration at NYCU, it began as a journey of transformation, guided by the philosophy of 易經 (I Ching). The process started with the creation of eight cardboard models, which were gradually merged—two by two—into four plaster models, and finally combined into a single cement form. Each stage brought a shift in materiality and expression, from the lightweight precision of cardboard to the dense solidity of plaster and cement.
