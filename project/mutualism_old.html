“Mutualism” explores an intertwined architectural concept, uniting a fashion workshop and an image studio in twin residential-commercial structures. A bicycle-friendly design accommodates New Hsinchu City’s commuting culture, with ramps seamlessly linking spaces. Functionally, the buildings foster mutual benefit, blending their respective industries while gradually transitioning between spaces through layered mezzanines.

From the exterior, the design appears as two distinct buildings with individual identities. However, a shared basement connects them, serving as a collaborative hub that fosters interaction while preserving autonomy. This balance of independence and connection is reflected in the complementary architectural forms.
