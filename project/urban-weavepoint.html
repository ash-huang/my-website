Located in Shihlin, Urban Weavepoint is a vibrant architectural development that embodies the essence of modern urban living. The design features a unique 3 by 3 intersecting bars layout, seamlessly integrating diverse zones: Stay Zone, Taste Zone, and Visual Zone. Complemented by preserved historic buildings and vibrant shopping areas, this project creates a dynamic hub that blends culture, entertainment, and community, reflecting the rich tapestry of contemporary city life.
